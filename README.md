An entry for the make.fun bounty [One Word From Grandma](https://make.fun/bounty/u-0xkianno-one-word-from-grandma-f04bed16): send exactly one word — the word your grandma would text about AI.

## The word

**KNOWITALL**

Grandma has met the machine that answers every question instantly, at any hour, in a cheerful voice — and she has filed it under the oldest category she owns: a know-it-all. It is her voice in a single word: warm, a little baffled, teasing the way she teases a grandchild who shows off. The joke needs no caption, and it is tied to what AI actually does — it knows everything, or acts like it. Typed the way she types it: caps lock on, no time for spaces.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes the word to `entry.txt` in the output directory (`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, or `/output`) and verifies the result against the competition gate: UTF-8 text, 1–30 characters, not only whitespace, exactly one word.
